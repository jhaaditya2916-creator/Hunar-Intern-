## Data Cleaning Methods

1. **Missing Values:** Missing values were handled using mode, median, or the label "Not provided", depending on the column.
2. **Invalid Values:** Invalid GPA entries were converted to numeric values, with resulting missing values filled using the median.
3. **Weight Standardization:** Text-based weight entries were converted to numeric values, and missing or invalid entries were handled.
4. **Text Standardization:** Leading and trailing spaces were removed from text columns.
5. **Inconsistent Text:** Capitalization was standardized in the `type_sports` column.
6. **Spelling and Punctuation:** The entries "Fotball" and "None." were corrected to "Football" and "None".
7. **Duplicate Check:** The dataset was checked for duplicate rows.
